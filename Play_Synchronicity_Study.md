# Play Synchronicity: The Deductive Geometric Mapping of Acausal Orderedness

## 1. The Historical Evolution of Jungian Categorical Statements
Standard dismissals of Synchronicity usually commit a category error by assuming that a human "psyche" is a mandatory requirement for an acausal coincidence. This study reconstructs Jung's thesis by tracing its text through its four core evolutionary phases:
1. **The 1929 Foundation:** Jung’s initial, localized definition delivered on Dec 4, 1929, which he later admitted was far too narrow.
2. **The 1950 Pauli Correspondence:** Eight months prior to his public monograph, Jung explicitly broke the psychic boundary in a letter to Wolfgang Pauli (Nov 30, 1950): *“I see no reason why synchronicity should always just be a coincidence of two psychic states or a psychic state and non psychic event. There may also possibly be coincidences of this kind between non-psychic events.”* (Atom and Archetype, p. 60).
3. **The 1951/1955 Eranos Monograph:** The introduction of the three standard categories.
4. **The 1957/1973 Editorial Shift:** The Joseph Campbell Eranos lecture edits utilized in the 1973 Princeton Edition, which obscured the crisp spacetime distinctions of the original formatting.
